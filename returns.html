Return Policy

Information about returns, exchanges, replacements, and refunds at Kattaleia's Candles.

Last Updated: [DATE]

At Kattaleia's Candles, we want you to be happy with your order. Because our products are handmade, certain conditions apply to returns, exchanges, and refunds.

Please read this policy before placing an order.

1. Handmade Products

Our candles are handmade, which means small variations in color, shape, texture, finish, or appearance may occur.

These natural differences are part of the handmade nature of our products and are not necessarily considered defects.

2. Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, please contact us as soon as possible.

Please provide:

  • Your name
  • Order details
  • A description of the issue
  • Clear photographs of the product and packaging, if applicable

We will review the issue and determine the appropriate solution.

Depending on the circumstances, we may offer:

  • A replacement
  • An exchange
  • A refund
  • Another appropriate solution

3. Returns

Because candles and handmade products may be prepared specifically for each order, returns may only be accepted under certain conditions.

To request a return, contact us within [NUMBER OF DAYS] days of receiving your order.

Returned products must generally:

  • Be unused
  • Be in their original condition
  • Be returned with their original packaging where applicable
  • Not have been damaged through customer use or handling

Products that have been used, burned, damaged, or altered may not be eligible for return.

4. Custom and Personalized Orders

Custom or personalized candles may not be eligible for return or exchange because they are made specifically according to the customer's request.

If a custom product arrives damaged or there is an error on our part, please contact us so we can review the situation.

5. Change of Mind

If you change your mind after placing an order, please contact us as soon as possible.

If your order has not yet been prepared or shipped, we may be able to make changes or cancel the order.

Once an order has entered production or has been shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible.

6. Refunds

If a refund is approved, we will communicate the refund process and expected timeframe to you.

The method and timing of the refund may depend on the original payment method.

Delivery fees may not be refundable unless the issue was caused by an error on our part or applicable law requires otherwise.

7. Exchanges and Replacements

If an exchange or replacement is approved, we will communicate the next steps with you.

Replacement availability may depend on product availability.

For handmade or customized products, the replacement may require additional preparation time.

8. Non-Returnable Situations

A return, exchange, or refund may not be available when:

  • The product has been used or burned.
  • The product was damaged after delivery due to improper handling.
  • Candle safety instructions were not followed.
  • The product was damaged through misuse.
  • The customer provided incorrect delivery information.
  • The customer changed their mind after the product was prepared, where applicable.
  • The product was customized specifically for the customer, unless the product is defective or there was an error on our part.

This list may not apply where applicable law provides additional consumer rights.

9. Delivery Issues

If an order is delayed, lost, or damaged during delivery, please contact us as soon as possible.

We will review the situation and work with you and the relevant delivery service to determine the appropriate solution.

Please make sure your delivery address and contact information are accurate when placing your order.

10. How to Request a Return or Refund

To request a return, exchange, replacement, or refund, contact us using one of the methods below.

Email: [EMAIL HERE]
WhatsApp: [WHATSAPP LINK HERE]
Phone: [PHONE NUMBER HERE]

Please include your order information and explain the issue clearly.

We may request photographs or additional information to help us review your request.

11. Processing Your Request

After receiving your request, we will review the information provided and communicate with you regarding the next steps.

Each request is reviewed based on the circumstances of the order and the condition of the product.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Return & Refund Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised Last Updated date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about returns, exchanges, refunds, or your order, please contact us.
